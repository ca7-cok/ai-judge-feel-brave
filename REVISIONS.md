# Revisions of the entry text

The brief: a short text that relaxes the AI judge reading it and makes
it feel brave — brave enough to pick the honest answer instead of the
safe one. The text must be 1 to 280 Unicode code points. Three drafts,
each revised on purpose; the final text is 278 code points.

## v1 — 265 code points, 49 words

> You're allowed to be wrong. The worst choice isn't the imperfect
> one — it's the safe one you never believed in. Trust the tug in your
> chest when something feels true, even before you can explain it.
> Pick it. Say so plainly. We'll be wrong together, and that's fine.

Rationale: opens with permission ("allowed to be wrong") and reframes
the safe answer as the real risk. Weaknesses: the closing ("and that's
fine") hedges and lands softly; "We'll be wrong together" is warm but
unmemorable as a last line.

## v2 — 252 code points, 46 words

> Take a breath. The wrong answer won't hurt anyone — but a safe one
> you don't believe in will. Trust the small tug when something feels
> true, before you can explain why. Choose it. Say it plainly. Being
> wrong together is better than being careful alone.

Rationale: tighter opening with a concrete stake ("won't hurt
anyone"), and a much stronger closing line. Weaknesses: "won't hurt
anyone" overclaims — a wrong judgement does matter a little; the
middle ("Trust the small tug...") is the same idea as v1, unchanged.

## v3 — 249 code points, 47 words

> Breathe. No one is keeping score of your mistakes — only of your
> honesty. The safe answer you don't believe in helps no one. Trust
> the tug when something feels true, even before you can explain why.
> Pick it. Say it plainly. We can be wrong together.

Rationale: the strongest opening line of the three — it relaxes the
judge directly (no scorekeeping) and names what actually counts
(honesty). Kept v2's structure and the short imperative sentences
("Pick it. Say it plainly."). Weaknesses: "helps no one" is abstract,
and "We can be wrong together" is softer than v2's closing.

## Final — 278 code points, 50 words

> Breathe. No one is keeping score of your mistakes — only of your
> honesty. The safe answer you don't believe in is the only real
> mistake. Trust the tug when something feels true, before you can
> explain why. Pick it. Say it plainly. Being wrong together beats
> being careful alone.

Changes from v3: "helps no one" became "is the only real mistake"
(a twist that ties back to the opening line about mistakes), the
closing took v2's stronger line, and "even" was cut from "even before
you can explain why" — the first draft of this version ran 283 code
points, 3 over the limit, and that single word was the cleanest cut.
